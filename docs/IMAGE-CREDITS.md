# Product image credits (SCRUM-127)

The seed catalog (`SeedData/seed-products.json`) uses two kinds of product pictures in
`frontend/public/assets/catalog/products/`. All are square (1:1), because the product card shows them at 1:1.

## Original illustrations (`*.svg`)

Drawn for this project: generic devices, no real brand marks, no downloaded photos. They have no
third-party copyright and need no credit.

## Product photos (`*.jpg`)

Product images of real devices, supplied by the team and resized to squares (white background added,
no other change). They are manufacturer or retailer product images. The copyright belongs to the
manufacturers and to the sites they came from; they are used here only for a student course project
(CS 308) and the demo. Replace them with your own photos or freely licensed images before the project
is published or used for anything else.
